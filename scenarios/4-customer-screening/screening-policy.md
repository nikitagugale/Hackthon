# Meridian Bank: Customer Screening Standard

**Version 3.1 · Owner: Head of Financial Crime Compliance**

## 1. What is screened

Every customer is screened against the sanctions and politically exposed person (PEP) lists at onboarding and at every periodic review. For a **company**, the screening covers the company **and its beneficial owner**.

## 2. Match levels

| Level | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed match** | The name, or a listed alias, matches **and** the date of birth matches. For a company, the beneficial owner matches a listed person's name and date of birth |
| **Potential match** | The name or an alias matches and **the date of birth is missing**, or the information is not enough to decide either way |
| **False positive** | The name matches but the date of birth is **clearly different** (more than two years apart) and nothing else links the two people |
| **No match** | Nothing links the customer to the list |

Names are compared allowing for spelling variants, transliteration, initials and word order.

## 3. What happens next

- **Confirmed match:** escalate to the Financial Crime Officer **the same day** and recommend that new activity on the account is held. The tool recommends. It never freezes an account.
- **Potential match:** ask the relationship manager for the missing information within five working days.
- **False positive:** record the reason, then send it for a **second person to confirm** (four-eyes). Only the second person can close it.
- **No match:** record that screening was done.

## 4. Adverse media

A media item counts against a customer only if it names the customer **and** at least one other detail agrees (for example age, city or occupation). A name alone is not enough. Record media that is linked, and say how severe it is.

## 5. Rules for every decision

- **Show the evidence:** the fields that were compared and what they said.
- **Never clear a customer automatically.** A person decides.
