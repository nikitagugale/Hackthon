# Federal Banking Authority: Circular 2026/14

**Outsourcing and third-party risk management.** Issued 3 November 2026. Applies to every licensed bank.
(The authority, the circular and the bank in this exercise are invented for teaching.)

## 1. Definitions

**1.1** A *material outsourcing* is an arrangement that (a) supports a critical or important function, or (b) costs more than **5%** of the bank's annual operating expenditure.

**1.2** A *subcontractor* is any party that the provider uses to deliver part of a material outsourcing.

## 2. Governance

**2.1** The board must approve an outsourcing policy and review it at least **annually**.

**2.2** The bank must keep a register of all outsourcing arrangements, update it at least **quarterly**, and give it to the Authority within **5 working days** of a request.

## 3. Due diligence and contracts

**3.1** Before a material outsourcing starts, the bank must complete documented due diligence that covers the provider's financial soundness, its security controls and its use of subcontractors.

**3.2** Every material outsourcing contract must give the bank **and the Authority** the right to audit the provider, including on-site access to the provider and its subcontractors.

**3.3** The contract must require the provider to obtain the bank's **prior written consent** before it subcontracts any part of a material outsourcing.

## 4. Resilience

**4.1** The bank must hold a documented **exit plan** for each material outsourcing and test it at least every two years.

**4.2** The bank must notify the Authority of a major incident at a provider within **4 hours** of becoming aware of it.

**4.3** A material outsourcing to a cloud provider needs a documented cloud risk assessment and an approval of the data location before it goes live.

**4.4** The bank should address any breach of these requirements by a provider **promptly**.

## 5. Data

**5.1** Customer data shared with a provider must be encrypted in transit and at rest.

## 6. Dates

**6.1** Sections 2 to 5 apply from **1 January 2027**, except paragraphs 4.1 and 4.3, which apply from **1 July 2027**.

**6.2** Each bank must send the Authority a gap assessment and an action plan by **31 March 2027**.
