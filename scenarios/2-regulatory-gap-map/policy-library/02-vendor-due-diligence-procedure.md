# Meridian Bank: Vendor Due Diligence Procedure

**Version 2.3 · Status: CURRENT · Effective 15 January 2026 · Owner: Head of Procurement**

## 1. When it applies

Due diligence is completed before any material outsourcing is signed.

## 2. What is checked

**2.1** Financial soundness: the last three years of accounts and a credit report.

**2.2** Information security controls: the provider's security certificate or the bank's security questionnaire.

**2.3** References from two existing clients.

## 3. Contract template

**3.1** Every material outsourcing contract uses the bank's template. The template gives **the bank** the right to audit the provider, on the provider's premises, with ten working days' notice.

**3.2** The template has a clause on confidentiality and one on termination for material breach.

## 4. Record keeping

The completed checklist is stored in the vendor file for the life of the contract and for five years afterwards.
