# Meridian Bank: Business Continuity and Incident Standard

**Version 5.0 · Status: CURRENT · Effective 1 June 2026 · Owner: Chief Risk Officer**

## 1. Incident classes

**1.1** Class 1 is a major incident: customer-facing service is unavailable for more than one hour, or customer data may be exposed.

**1.2** Class 2 and Class 3 are handled inside the affected team.

## 2. Reporting

**2.1** The incident manager classifies an incident within one hour of detection.

**2.2** A Class 1 incident at **a third-party provider** is reported to the regulator within **24 hours** of its classification.

**2.3** A written report follows within ten working days.

## 3. Continuity testing

**3.1** Business continuity plans for critical services are tested **every year**.

**3.2** The results are reported to the Risk Committee.
