# Meridian Bank: Information Security Policy

**Version 7.2 · Status: CURRENT · Effective 1 April 2026 · Owner: Chief Information Security Officer**

## 1. Access

**1.1** Access to systems follows the principle of least privilege and is reviewed every six months.

## 2. Encryption

**2.1** Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

**2.2** This applies to customer data **shared with third parties**, as well as to data held inside the bank.

## 3. Logging

**3.1** Access to customer data is logged, and logs are kept for twelve months.
