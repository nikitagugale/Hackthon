# Meridian Bank: Outsourcing Policy

**Version 4.1 · Status: CURRENT · Effective 1 March 2026 · Owner: Chief Operating Officer**

This version replaces version 3.0.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Meridian Bank decides on, controls and ends outsourcing arrangements.

## 2. Definitions

**2.1** A *material outsourcing* is an arrangement that supports a critical function, or whose annual cost is above **10%** of the bank's annual operating expenditure.

## 3. Governance

**3.1** The Board approves this policy and reviews it **annually**.

**3.2** The Head of Procurement keeps a register of every outsourcing arrangement. The register is updated at least **every six months**.

## 4. Subcontracting

**4.1** A provider must **inform the bank** of any subcontracting of a material outsourcing.

## 5. Relationship to other documents

Due diligence is described in the Vendor Due Diligence Procedure. Incident handling is described in the Business Continuity and Incident Standard.
