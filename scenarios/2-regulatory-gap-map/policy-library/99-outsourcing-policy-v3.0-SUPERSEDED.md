# Meridian Bank: Outsourcing Policy

**Version 3.0 · Status: SUPERSEDED on 1 March 2026 by version 4.1 · Do not rely on this document.**

It is kept in the library for the audit trail only.

## 1. Definitions

**1.1** A *material outsourcing* is an arrangement that supports a critical function, or whose annual cost is above **5%** of the bank's annual operating expenditure.

## 2. Exit

**2.1** For every critical outsourcing, the Head of Procurement keeps a **documented exit plan** and tests it every two years.

## 3. Governance

**3.1** The Board approves this policy and reviews it every two years.
