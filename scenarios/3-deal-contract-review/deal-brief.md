# Project Kestrel: what the buyer cares about

**Buyer:** Corvane Holdings. **Target:** Brightwater Logistics. **Review date:** 20 November 2026. **Signing target:** 18 December 2026.

## Context

- Aurelia Retail is about 38% of Brightwater's revenue.
- Corvane already serves **Northgate Foods**, a direct competitor of Aurelia, and plans to grow that business.
- The buyer wants to keep every customer after the deal closes.

## The topics to check in every contract

| Topic | Why the buyer cares |
|---|---|
| Change of control | Can the other side leave or renegotiate because Brightwater is sold? |
| Termination | How and when can the contract end, and with what notice? |
| Exclusivity and non-compete | Does it restrict who Brightwater, or its new owner, can serve? |
| Liability | Is liability capped? What are the carve-outs? |
| Renewal | Does it renew by itself, and has the notice date already passed? |
| Assignment | Can the contract be transferred to a new owner without consent? |
| Data protection | Does the contract deal with personal data, and who is responsible? |

## Severity scale

- **High:** could change the price, block the deal or lose a major customer.
- **Medium:** needs a negotiated fix or a specific warranty from the seller.
- **Low:** note it and move on.

## Rules for the review

- Every flag needs the **clause number and a quote**.
- If a topic is **not in a contract**, say so. Do not infer it.
