# Contract B: Cold Chain Services Agreement, Pinecrest Pharma Distribution Pvt Ltd and Brightwater Logistics Ltd

*Extract for the data room. Dated 15 January 2025.*

## 2. Term

**2.1** This Agreement starts on 15 January 2025 and continues for three years, to 14 January 2028.

## 5. Services

**5.1** Brightwater shall transport and store Pinecrest's temperature-controlled medicines at 2 to 8 degrees Celsius and record the temperature every ten minutes.

## 9. Liability

**9.1** Subject to clause 9.3, each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim.

**9.2** Neither party is liable for loss of profit.

**9.3** Brightwater's liability for any breach of clause 11 (Data and security) or for any loss of patient or customer data is **unlimited**.

## 11. Data and security

**11.1** Brightwater shall protect Pinecrest's patient and customer data with industry standard security and tell Pinecrest of a breach within 24 hours.

## 12. Termination

**12.1** Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on **30 days'** written notice.

**12.2** Either party may terminate for material breach that is not fixed within 30 days of notice.

## 15. Assignment

**15.1** Brightwater may assign this Agreement to a group company on written notice to Pinecrest.

## Schedule 2: Service levels and exit

1. Delivery on time: at least 98% of consignments each month.
2. Temperature excursions: no more than 0.5% of consignments.
3. Service credits: 2% of the monthly fee for each full percentage point below the target.
4. **Termination for convenience under this Agreement requires 90 days' written notice.**

## Schedule 3: Contacts and forms

Escalation contacts are held by the account managers of each party.
