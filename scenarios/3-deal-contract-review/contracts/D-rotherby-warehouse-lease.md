# Contract D: Warehouse Lease, Rotherby Estates Ltd (landlord) and Brightwater Logistics Ltd (tenant)

*Extract for the data room. Dated 1 October 2022.*

## 2. Term

**2.1** The lease runs for nine years, from 1 October 2022 to 30 September 2031.

## 3. Rent

**3.1** Annual rent is 9,600,000, paid quarterly in advance.

**3.2** The rent increases by 5% on each anniversary of the start date.

## 7. Use

**7.1** The tenant may use the premises only for warehousing and distribution.

## 9. Assignment and control

**9.1** The tenant may not assign or sublet the premises without the landlord's prior written consent, which the landlord shall not unreasonably withhold.

**9.2** A **change in control of the tenant** is treated as an assignment and needs the landlord's prior written consent under clause 9.1.

## 11. Indemnity

**11.1** The tenant shall indemnify the landlord for any loss that results from the tenant's use of the premises.

## 13. Termination

**13.1** The landlord may end the lease if rent is more than 60 days late.

**13.2** The tenant may end the lease early only at the end of the fifth year, on 12 months' notice.
