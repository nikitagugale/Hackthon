# Contract A: Master Services Agreement, Aurelia Retail Ltd and Brightwater Logistics Ltd

*Extract for the data room. Dated 1 April 2024.*

## 1. Definitions

**1.1** *Affiliate* means, for a party, any entity that controls it, is controlled by it, or is under common control with it, now or in the future.

**1.2** *Competitor* means any business that competes with Aurelia in retail, including **Northgate Foods** and its affiliates.

**1.3** *Change of Control* means any transaction by which a person, alone or with others, acquires more than 50% of the voting shares of Brightwater or the power to direct its management.

## 2. Term

**2.1** This Agreement starts on 1 April 2024 and continues for five years, to 31 March 2029, unless it ends earlier under clause 14.

## 6. Exclusivity

**6.1** During the term and for twelve months afterwards, Brightwater shall not provide logistics services to a Competitor in the Territory (India), **and shall procure that each of its Affiliates does not do so**.

## 9. Liability

**9.1** Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim.

**9.2** Nothing limits liability for death, fraud or wilful default.

## 11. Data protection

**11.1** Brightwater acts as a processor of Aurelia's customer data. It shall follow Aurelia's written instructions, keep the data secure and tell Aurelia of any breach within 48 hours.

## 14. Termination

**14.1** Aurelia may terminate for convenience on 180 days' written notice.

**14.2** Aurelia may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice if Brightwater undergoes a Change of Control without Aurelia's prior written consent.

**14.3** Either party may terminate for material breach that is not fixed within 30 days of notice.

## 16. Assignment

**16.1** Brightwater may not assign or subcontract this Agreement without Aurelia's prior written consent.
