# Contract C: Fleet Supply and Maintenance Agreement, Halden Fleet Services Ltd and Brightwater Logistics Ltd

*Extract for the data room. Dated 1 January 2024.*

## 2. Term and renewal

**2.1** The initial term is three years, from 1 January 2024 to **31 December 2026**.

**2.2** After the initial term this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of **three years**, unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least **120 days** before the end of the current term.

## 3. Fees

**3.1** Brightwater pays an annual fleet fee of **4,200,000** in equal monthly instalments.

**3.2** If Brightwater ends this Agreement before the end of a term, other than for Halden's uncured breach, it pays **80% of the fees** that remain for the term.

## 5. Service levels

**5.1** Halden shall keep 95% of the fleet available each month and shall replace a failed vehicle within 24 hours.

## 8. Liability

**8.1** Each party's total liability is limited to the annual fleet fee.

## 10. Termination

**10.1** Either party may terminate for material breach that is not fixed within 30 days of notice.

## 12. Assignment

**12.1** Either party may assign this Agreement to a group company on written notice.
