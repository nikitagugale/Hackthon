# Approval matrix: Halvorsen Industrial Group (manual journal entries)

| Amount of the entry | Who must approve |
|---|---|
| Up to and including 500,000 | Finance Manager (`m.rao`) |
| Above 500,000 | Controller (`c.sharma`) |

- Accountants (`a.mehta`, `r.iyer`, `s.kapoor`, `d.nair`, `p.verma`) prepare entries. They cannot approve.
- An approver must not approve an entry that they prepared.
- Automated postings from the sales and purchase interfaces use the approver `AUTO`.
- Amounts are in Indian rupees.
