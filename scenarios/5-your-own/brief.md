# Scenario 5: Your own enterprise problem

## The situation

You know a problem from your own work, or from a project, where AI could help a professional team: in audit, tax, risk, deals, consulting, operations, cyber or technology. Bring that instead of one of the four.

## What you do

Describe it in one line in the chat by 09:30: **who it is for, and what you will show.** You can start straight away.

## The rules for this one

- It must be a **real kind of work problem**, with a user who would care: a reviewer, a manager, a client.
- Use **made-up data that looks real**. Never use client data, personal data or anything confidential.
- You need your **own sample data and your own test cases**. Nobody will give them to you.
- It must be a **small web app** that you can demo live in five minutes. One page is enough.

## How it is scored

The same rubric as everyone else. The three "scenario catches" are replaced by three criteria that are yours to earn:

- **A real problem, well framed** (5): who it is for, what a good result looks like, and why it matters.
- **A clear test of success** (5): you decided how you would know it works, before you built it, and you used it.
- **Realistic data** (5): the data has awkward cases in it, and you show how the tool deals with them.

## Ideas worth trying

They are ideas, not requirements.

- Pick a problem where **being wrong has a cost**, so that your checks matter.
- Put **one trap in your own data** and show that you catch it.
- Decide where a person must stay in charge, and show it.
